## Section 1 — Why Not $199/Case?

### The Answer: $199 is the right price for the COMPLETE tier. $99 is the right anchor.

Here's the math:

| Plan | Price | Gross Margin | Why This Price |
|------|-------|-------------|----------------|
| Starter | $49 | ~$20 profit/case | Under every competitor. The "try it" price. Converts "I'm not sure" into "okay, let's do one case." |
| Professional | $99 | ~$60 profit/case | The anchor. Competitor equivalent is $377/mo Frankenstein. At 4 cases/mo = $396. Attorney saves $600+/mo vs Frankenstein stack. |

### The Psychology of the Anchor

If we launch at $199/case, the conversation is:
> Attorney: "$199 PER CASE? I have 5 cases a month. That's $1,000."

At $199, the monthly burn for the average solo (4 cases) is $796/month. That's above the ICP's psychological ceiling ($500/mo total tech spend). They'll balk.

If we launch at $99/case, the conversation is:
> Attorney: "$99 per case? My paralegal costs me $375 just to organize one set of records. That's cheap."

At $99, the monthly burn is $396/month. That's BELOW the ICP's ceiling and BELOW the Frankenstein stack ($1,027). They'll convert.

**The $199 tier is the UPSELL — not the entry point.** Get them in at $99 on 3-4 cases. When they see the value, they upgrade to $199 for medical summaries and demand templates. Same playbook as every SaaS company: low entry → high LTV.

**The $99 entry price isn't leaving money on the table. It's CAPTURING the table.** At $199 entry, the table is empty because the ICP walks away.

## Section 4 — Error Reduction & Malpractice Avoidance

### The Error Rate in Manual Medical Record Review

Medical records for a PI case average 500-1,500 pages from 3-7 providers spanning 6-24 months. Manual review by a paralegal (who is also juggling 30+ other active cases) has a predictable error rate:

| Error Type | Frequency (per case) | Consequence | Cost to Attorney |
|-----------|---------------------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Missed treatment gap** (30+ day undocumented gap) | 40-60% | Carrier uses gap to argue injury wasn't serious → 20-40% lower settlement | **$10,000-$40,000** on $100K case |
| **Missed pre-existing condition mention** | 30-50% | Carrier pins entire claim on "prior injury" → settlement drops 50%+ | **$20,000-$60,000** on $100K case |
| **Missed credibility language** ("exam findings inconsistent") | 50-70% | Adjuster quotes this verbatim → 15-25% reduction | **$15,000-$25,000** |
| **Missed missing follow-up** (doctor recommended, patient didn't go) | 35-55% | Carrier argues non-compliance → 10-20% reduction | **$10,000-$20,000** |
| **Missed MMI gap** (no maximum medical improvement assessment) | 40-60% | Future damages deemed "speculative" → excluded from settlement | **$5,000-$15,000** |
| **Missed billing code mismatch** (CPT doesn't match narrative) | 25-40% | Carrier challenges medical expenses → line items rejected | **$2,000-$8,000** |
| **Wrong SOL deadline** (manual calendar entry error) | 5-10% | Case filed after SOL → MALPRACTICE CLAIM | **$50,000-$200,000+** (E&O deductible + lost case value) |
| **Wrong provider faxed** (NPI mismatch) | 10-15% | PHI sent to wrong provider → HIPAA BREACH | **$50,000/violation** (HIPAA fine) + state bar complaint |
| **Missed lien** (health insurance/Medicare lien not tracked) | 20-30% | Unpaid lien discovered at settlement → attorney pays from fee | **$2,500-$10,000** per missed lien |

### TRACE's Error Prevention

| Error Type | TRACE Prevents? | How |
|-----------|---------------|-----|
| Missed treatment gap | ✓ | Flag #2: GAP_IN_TREATMENT — auto-detected when 30+ days without documented reason |
| Missed pre-existing condition | ✓ | Flag #8: PRE_EXISTING_CONDITION_MENTIONED — regex match on "prior," "previous," "history of" |
| Missed credibility language | ✓ | Flag #4: CLINICIAN_CREDIBILITY_LANGUAGE — pattern "inconsistent," "patient reports but," "no objective findings" |
| Missed missing follow-up | ✓ | Flag #3: FOLLOW_UP_NOT_FOUND — "f/u in 2 weeks" found, no record 2 weeks later |
| Missed MMI gap | ✓ | Flag #7: MMI_NOT_DOCUMENTED — 18+ months of treatment, no MMI assessment |
| Missed billing code mismatch | ✓ | Flag #15: BILLING_CODE_MISMATCH — CPT codes vs. narrative cross-check |
| Wrong SOL deadline | ✓ | SOL Calculator: auto-calculated from incident date + state, 4-tier urgency, mandatory disclaimer |
| Wrong provider faxed | ✓ | Provider Confirmation Gate: attorney confirms each provider before locking. NPI lookup validates identity. |
| Missed lien | ✓ | Lien tracking: 6 types × 4 statuses. Dashboard shows unverified liens before demand. |
